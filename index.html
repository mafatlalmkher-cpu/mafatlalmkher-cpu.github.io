ARIEL UNIVERSITYResearch in practice · January 2022–present+

Postdoctoral research in Prof. Elyashiv Drori’s Grape and Wine Research Group connects plant regeneration, molecular diagnostics and conservation propagation.

Grapevine propagation from explant to field

I have established a propagation protocol for indigenous grapevine varieties, covering culture initiation, shoot multiplication, rooting and acclimatization; a manuscript is in preparation. My work also includes budbreak and explant selection, shoot-tip culture, and the maintenance of plants in Ariel University’s germplasm collection.

Plant virus diagnostics

I work with RNA extraction from leaf and woody tissue, cDNA synthesis, gradient PCR and gel electrophoresis, alongside PCR and RT-qPCR indexing. This supports comparison of field and tissue-culture material and the evaluation of sanitation workflows.

Characterizing a living grapevine collection

I contribute to cultivar documentation using OIV descriptors, powdery mildew screening, photographic records of fruit bunches and tracking fruit development.

Hawthorn seed biology and propagation

Research on spiny and red hawthorn addresses seed dormancy, germination and in vitro shoot multiplication. The work includes evaluating rooting, acclimatization and cutting-based approaches, with the aim of supporting nursery production and conservation.

Cypress conservation biotechnology

My work on Cupressus atlantica includes explant selection, aseptic culture establishment, callus and shoot-culture trials, vegetative propagation and sampling for molecular studies. Somatic embryogenesis and acclimatization are areas of protocol development. A related KKL-JNF project, begun in February 2026, focuses on Cupressus dupreziana and Hesperocyparis guadalupensis.

Mentoring and scientific communication

I support MSc research in the group and contribute to proposal development and scientific manuscripts. For the published wild-grapevine study, my documented contributions include original drafting, revision and scientific visualization.
